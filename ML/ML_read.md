# 🏦 Home Credit Risk Analysis & Credit Default Prediction

## 📌 Project Overview

Financial institutions face a significant challenge when deciding whether to approve or reject loan applications. Making inaccurate lending decisions can lead to increased financial losses due to customer defaults, while overly strict approval policies may result in missed business opportunities.

This project focuses on analyzing customer financial and behavioral data to better understand the factors associated with credit default risk. Multiple data sources were integrated, including application information, credit bureau history, previous loan applications, POS cash balances, and installment payment behavior.

A comprehensive data engineering and feature engineering pipeline was developed to transform raw customer records into meaningful risk indicators. These engineered features were then used to build machine learning models capable of estimating the probability of customer default.

The project combines:

* 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
* 🛠️ Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
* 🔄 Feature Engineering
* 🤖 Machine Learning Modeling
* 📈 Credit Risk Assessment

The final solution provides a data-driven approach for identifying high-risk customers and supporting better lending decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Goals

The primary business objectives of this project are:

### 💳 Improve Credit Risk Assessment

Identify customers who are more likely to default on their loans before approval decisions are made.

### 📉 Reduce Financial Losses

Minimize losses caused by high-risk borrowers by improving the quality of risk evaluation.

### 🏦 Support Lending Decisions

Provide an analytical framework that helps financial institutions make more informed loan approval decisions.

### 📊 Understand Customer Behavior

Analyze repayment patterns, credit history, debt burden, and previous borrowing behavior to uncover important risk indicators.

### ⚡ Automate Risk Evaluation

Develop a machine learning-based scoring system capable of evaluating applicants at scale and providing default probability estimates.

### 📈 Enhance Portfolio Quality

Help lenders maintain a healthier loan portfolio by balancing customer acquisition with risk management.
